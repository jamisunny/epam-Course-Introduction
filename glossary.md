• **Token**: The basic unit of text—such as a word, part of a word, or punctuation mark—that an AI model reads and processes.

• **Embedding**: A numerical list (vector) that represents the meaning of text, images, or other data, allowing AI to measure how similar concepts are.

• **Context window**: The maximum amount of text (measured in tokens) an AI model can hold in its short-term memory at one time during a conversation or task.

• **RAG**: A technique that lets an AI pull fresh, external information from a database or the web before generating an answer, improving accuracy.

• **Hallucinations**: Instances where an AI confidently generates false, nonsensical, or fabricated information presented as fact.

• **AI-Native Tool**s: Software applications built from the ground up around artificial intelligence as their core functionality, rather than adding AI as a secondary feature.

• **Lossy Compression**: A data compression method that reduces file size by permanently discarding less important details. It's irreversible compression, is the class of data compression methods that uses inexact approximations and partial data discarding to represent the content.

• **LLM**: A massive AI system trained on huge amounts of text to understand, summarize, and generate human-like language.

• **AI assistant**: A software program powered by AI that interacts with users via text or voice to answer questions, automate tasks, and provide recommendations.

• **RLHF**: A training method where human reviewers rank AI responses to teach the model how to give safer, more helpful, and more natural answers.

• **ML**: A branch of artificial intelligence where computer systems learn patterns from data to make predictions or decisions without being explicitly programmed for every rule.

• **Transformer Model**: A neural network architecture that uses an "attention" mechanism to weigh the importance of different words in a sentence simultaneously, forming the backbone of modern LLMs.

• **NLP**: A field of AI focused on enabling computers to understand, interpret, and generate human language.
